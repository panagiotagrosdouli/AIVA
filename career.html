2. How matching works

We compare job titles, employers and short descriptions with your desired role, skills and CV keywords. More relevant keyword overlaps are ranked higher.

🌍 Jobs come from a public international remote-work feed when synchronization succeeds. Not every country or job market is covered.

🔒 Nothing is sent when you enter your CV. Text is only retained in your open browser tab. Do not paste sensitive personal data.

⚠️ A high match score does not mean you qualify or that an employer will hire you. Always verify location restrictions, requirements and whether the position remains open.
