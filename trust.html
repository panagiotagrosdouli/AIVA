About AIVA

AIVA is an independent opportunity discovery project maintained through its public repository. It brings together job listings, academic funding directories and career organization tools. The current service is a free beta; it does not charge candidates or submit applications on their behalf.

Where listings come from

Job search reads periodically imported data from Himalayas, public employer recruitment boards and available European feeds. We show the source name when supplied, retain the original application URL and exclude entries with known past expiry dates. Duplicate URLs are removed. An imported listing is not independently verified as open, legitimate or suitable for every applicant.

Checking published feed metadata…

Study and funding entries are links to official directories, not a database of individually verified open awards. Check eligibility, deadlines and location restrictions on the source website.

How career matching works

Career Passport compares job text with the role, skills and CV keywords you provide. It ranks overlaps using rules, not semantic AI or a prediction of hiring success. Missing skills, eligibility, experience requirements and location restrictions must still be checked with the employer.

Privacy and data handling

CV parsing runs in your browser with third-party parsing libraries loaded from external providers. The CV text is not uploaded to an AIVA server by Career Passport. Saved jobs, search terms, applications and salary comparisons are stored locally in your browser. Shared devices may expose these records to other users of the same browser profile.

Export saved jobs and tracker records using their export controls. To remove browser-held AIVA records, use the remove controls on each page or clear this site's storage in your browser settings. Clearing site data may also remove other records on the same GitHub Pages origin.

Hosting and external services may receive ordinary network request information. External application pages have their own privacy practices. Cloud account features require backend configuration; this page does not promise cloud deletion, security certification or email alerts.

Apply safely

Check the employer's official website and confirm the job details before sharing documents. Be cautious of requests for upfront fees, bank credentials or identity documents before a legitimate hiring process. Never rely on an AIVA source label alone as proof of legitimacy.

Report a listing through GitHub ↗ (GitHub account required; reports may be public. Do not include personal information or CVs.)

Help & feedback

For problems with search, broken links or suggestions, use the public project issue tracker. Do not post private account details or sensitive information. A dedicated private support channel and guaranteed response times are not currently available.
